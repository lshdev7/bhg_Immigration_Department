Identity Cards

The Baekhyeon Hwarang Smart Identity Card is issued to Baekhyeon Hwarang residents. Applicants must attend the Immigration Department in person for the capture of biometric data. You can make an appointment online for the application or collection of a Smart Identity Card.

Book Appointment to Apply for Baekhyeon Hwarang Smart Identity Card

Who Should Apply

  • Persons who have reached the age of 11 and are required to register for an identity card
  • Holders who need a replacement of a lost, stolen or damaged identity card
  • Holders of an identity card who need to change particulars

What to Bring

  • Your appointment reference number
  • Documents proving your identity and right of abode (e.g. birth certificate)
  • Your existing identity card, if any

Fees

The first registration for an identity card is free of charge. A fee of BH$370 is payable for replacement of a lost or damaged card.
