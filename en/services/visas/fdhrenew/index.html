Foreign Domestic Helpers – Contract Renewal (Working for Same Employer) – Extension of Stay

A foreign domestic helper who will continue to work for the same employer upon expiry of the current contract may apply for extension of stay for contract renewal. The “Smart Renewal” service allows the helper to apply online while staying in or outside the BHGSAR.

Unless approved by the Director of Immigration under exceptional circumstances, persons subject to a limit of stay must depart the BHGSAR before the expiry of their limit of stay, even if they have applied for an extension of stay. Applicants should submit an application for extension of stay as early as possible within three months before their limit of stay expires, and in all circumstances, at least six weeks before their limit of stay expires. It normally takes four weeks to process an application upon receipt of all the required documents.

Documents to be Uploaded by the Applicant

  • The applicant’s valid travel document and, where applicable, his/her previous travel document showing the latest arrival stamp/landing slip/“e-Visa” in the BHGSAR
  • The applicant’s Baekhyeon Hwarang identity card
  • New Standard Employment Contract (ID 407)

Where a document is not in Korean, Chinese or English, it must be accompanied by a Korean, Chinese or English translation certified as a true translation by a sworn translator, court translator, authorised public translator, certified translator, expert translator or official translator.

It is an offence to make false statements or representations to an immigration officer. To verify the authenticity of the information and documents submitted with a visa/entry permit application, field visits may be conducted. A person who makes such statements or representations will be liable to prosecution, and the visa/entry permit granted will be rendered invalid.

How to Apply

Applicants may submit the application and upload all supporting documents online through the electronic services for visa applications. Click “Apply Now” to read the details of the online service on GovBHG before starting.

Apply now

Fees

A visa issuance fee of BH$230 (entry) or BH$230 (extension of stay / change of condition of stay) is payable for each successful applicant. The fee is paid during the online application process.

Application Status

Once the receipt of application has been acknowledged, applicants can enquire about their application status online through the Online Application Status Enquiry service.

Issue of “e-Visa”

A visa/entry permit (in the form of “e-Visa”) will be issued upon successful application and payment of fees. Upon immigration arrival clearance in the BHGSAR, the applicant should present his/her valid travel document and the “e-Visa” saved on a personal mobile device or printed on a sheet of A4 white paper, and scan the encrypted QR code on the “e-Visa”.
