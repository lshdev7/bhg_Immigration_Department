Immigration Guidelines for Entry to the Baekhyeon Hwarang Special Administrative Region of the Republic of Korea

This page provides general guidelines on entry to the BHGSAR for leisure, social or business visits, employment, investment, training, study and residence.

Visit

Visitors should hold a valid travel document, adequate funds and an onward / return ticket. Visitors may not take up employment, establish or join in business, or study.

Employment and investment

Persons who wish to work or invest in the BHGSAR should apply under the appropriate talent, employment or investment scheme before arrival.

Study

Non-local students must obtain a student visa / entry permit through a local sponsoring institution.

Residence as dependants

Dependants of BHG residents may apply to join their sponsor in the BHGSAR under the dependant policy.

While each application is determined on its individual merits, an applicant should meet normal immigration requirements (such as holding a valid travel document with adequate returnability to his/her country of residence or citizenship; be of clear criminal record and raise no security or criminal concerns to the BHGSAR; have no likelihood of becoming a burden on the BHGSAR, etc.) as well as the relevant specific eligibility criteria before he/she may be considered for the grant of a visa/entry permit. The eligibility criteria may be subject to change from time to time without prior notice.

Last revision date: November 2026
