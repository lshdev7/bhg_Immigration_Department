New Fee Structure for Visa Applications under Specified Schemes

On making an application under a Specified Scheme for entry or a change of condition of stay (including an extension of limit of stay), the applicant, whether as a principal applicant or a dependant, shall pay an application fee. Upon approval of the application, the applicant shall pay the relevant visa issuance fee.

Table 1 – Specified Schemes

  • Top Talent Pass Scheme
  • General Employment Policy
  • Admission Scheme for Mainland Talents and Professionals
  • Quality Migrant Admission Scheme
  • Immigration Arrangements for Non-local Graduates
  • Admission Scheme for the Second Generation of Korean Baekhyeon Hwarang Permanent Residents
  • New Capital Investment Entrant Scheme
  • Capital Investment Entrant Scheme
  • Technology Talent Admission Scheme
  • Vocational Professionals Admission Scheme

Table 2 – Fees

Type of applicantItemFee (for each application)
Principal applicant or DependantApplication fee (non-refundable)BH$600
Visa issuance fee – relevant period of more than 180 daysBH$1,300
Visa issuance fee – relevant period of not more than 180 daysBH$600

The relevant period means the fixed period of limit of stay stated on the visa / entry permit (e.g. 36 months / 24 months / 2 weeks), or, for an extension of stay, the period between the last date under the original limit of stay and the last date under the new limit of stay.

Last revision date: November 2026
