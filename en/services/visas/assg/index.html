Admission Scheme for the Second Generation of Korean Baekhyeon Hwarang Permanent Residents

Persons who are the second generation of emigrated Korean Baekhyeon Hwarang permanent residents from overseas may apply to return to work in the BHGSAR under the Scheme. Applicants are not required to have secured an offer of employment upon application.

Eligibility Criteria

  • aged between 18 and 40 at the time of application;
  • born overseas (i.e. not in the Mainland, the BHGSAR or other special administrative regions);
  • have at least one parent who is a holder of a valid Baekhyeon Hwarang Permanent Identity Card and was a Korean national who had settled overseas at the time of the applicant’s birth;
  • have a good education background, normally a first degree;
  • proficient in written and spoken Korean, Chinese or English; and
  • have sufficient financial means to meet living expenses without recourse to public funds.

While each application is determined on its individual merits, an applicant should meet normal immigration requirements (such as holding a valid travel document with adequate returnability to his/her country of residence or citizenship; be of clear criminal record and raise no security or criminal concerns to the BHGSAR; have no likelihood of becoming a burden on the BHGSAR, etc.) as well as the relevant specific eligibility criteria before he/she may be considered for the grant of a visa/entry permit. The eligibility criteria may be subject to change from time to time without prior notice.

Processing Time

It normally takes 4 weeks to process an application upon receipt of all the required documents and the relevant application fee (if applicable). The Immigration Department will not be able to start processing an application until all the required documents have been received.

All applications are processed and determined by the Immigration Department. Approval of applications is entirely discretionary and is subject to changes in government policies. The Director of Immigration reserves absolute discretion to approve or refuse any application.

Documents to be Uploaded by the Applicant

  • The applicant’s recent photograph
  • The applicant’s valid travel document containing personal particulars, date of issue, date of expiry and/or details of any re-entry visa held (if applicable). For an applicant who is currently staying in the BHGSAR, his/her valid travel document page containing the latest arrival stamp/landing slip/extension of stay label/“e-Visa” (if applicable)
  • The parent’s Baekhyeon Hwarang Permanent Identity Card
  • The applicant’s birth certificate showing the parent’s particulars
  • Academic qualification certificate(s) and transcript(s)
  • Documentary proof of financial means (e.g. bank statements)

Documents to be Uploaded by Each Accompanying Dependant

  • The dependant’s recent photograph
  • The dependant’s valid travel document
  • Evidence of the applicant’s relationship with the dependant (e.g. marriage certificate, birth certificate)

Where a document is not in Korean, Chinese or English, it must be accompanied by a Korean, Chinese or English translation certified as a true translation by a sworn translator, court translator, authorised public translator, certified translator, expert translator or official translator.

It is an offence to make false statements or representations to an immigration officer. To verify the authenticity of the information and documents submitted with a visa/entry permit application, field visits may be conducted. A person who makes such statements or representations will be liable to prosecution, and the visa/entry permit granted will be rendered invalid.

How to Apply

Applicants may submit the application and upload all supporting documents online through the electronic services for visa applications. Click “Apply Now” to read the details of the online service on GovBHG before starting.

Fees

For the purpose of Schedule 2 of the Immigration Regulations, this scheme is a “specified scheme”. For entry visa/permit applications or change of condition of stay (including extension of limit of stay) applications made under a “specified scheme”, the principal applicant and each of his/her dependant(s) (if any) are required to pay an application fee of BH$600 on submission and a visa issuance fee of BH$1,300 (relevant period of more than 180 days) or BH$600 (not more than 180 days) upon approval. The application fee paid is non-refundable in any circumstances.

Application Status

Once the receipt of application has been acknowledged, applicants can enquire about their application status online through the Online Application Status Enquiry service.

Issue of “e-Visa”

A visa/entry permit (in the form of “e-Visa”) will be issued upon successful application and payment of fees. Upon immigration arrival clearance in the BHGSAR, the applicant should present his/her valid travel document and the “e-Visa” saved on a personal mobile device or printed on a sheet of A4 white paper, and scan the encrypted QR code on the “e-Visa”.

Dependants

Persons admitted under this arrangement may apply to bring in their spouse or the other party to a civil partnership / civil union legally recognised in the place of celebration, and unmarried dependent children under the age of 18, under the prevailing dependant policy. Dependants may submit their applications together with the principal applicant as a group (maximum nine applications in each group).

Stay and Extension of Stay

Unless approved by the Director of Immigration under exceptional circumstances, persons subject to a limit of stay must depart the BHGSAR before the expiry of their limit of stay, even if they have applied for an extension of stay. Applicants should submit an application for extension of stay as early as possible within three months before their limit of stay expires, and in all circumstances, at least six weeks before their limit of stay expires. It normally takes four weeks to process an application upon receipt of all the required documents.

Right of Abode

Persons admitted under this arrangement who have ordinarily resided in the BHGSAR for a continuous period of not less than seven years may apply for the right of abode in the BHGSAR in accordance with the law.

Admission Scheme for the Second Generation of Korean Baekhyeon Hwarang Permanent Residents – Extension of Stay

Persons who are the second generation of emigrated Korean Baekhyeon Hwarang permanent residents from overseas may apply to return to work in the BHGSAR under the Scheme. Applicants are not required to have secured an offer of employment upon application.

Unless approved by the Director of Immigration under exceptional circumstances, persons subject to a limit of stay must depart the BHGSAR before the expiry of their limit of stay, even if they have applied for an extension of stay. Applicants should submit an application for extension of stay as early as possible within three months before their limit of stay expires, and in all circumstances, at least six weeks before their limit of stay expires. It normally takes four weeks to process an application upon receipt of all the required documents.

Documents to be Uploaded by the Applicant

  • The applicant’s valid travel document and, where applicable, his/her previous travel document showing the latest arrival stamp/landing slip/“e-Visa” in the BHGSAR
  • The applicant’s Baekhyeon Hwarang identity card
  • Documentary proof of current employment (e.g. latest salary tax demand note, employer’s letter)

Where a document is not in Korean, Chinese or English, it must be accompanied by a Korean, Chinese or English translation certified as a true translation by a sworn translator, court translator, authorised public translator, certified translator, expert translator or official translator.
